## S8 — Bots: dash-at-nearest AI, yield-to-human, practice mode (2026-07-23)

- Moved `laneDistance` (Cross Slash's beam-reach math) from `ougi.ts` into `math.ts` as a shared, exported helper — the bot AI needed the same "how far can I go along this axis before hitting a wall/obstacle" logic and duplicating it would have let the two drift apart.
- New `packages/server/src/bot.ts`: `decideBotCommand(state, ninja)` — dash-at-nearest AI, one decision per simulation callback (same cadence real client input arrives at). A bot holds (`charging: true`) until its TP covers at least half a full-power dash, fires its Ougi outright the instant the meter is full, and otherwise picks whichever of the two candidate cardinal axes toward the nearest live target has clearer line-of-sight via `laneDistance` (basic obstacle avoidance) before releasing a `LaunchCommand` at that axis and power. Bot ninja ids are namespaced (`bot:1`, `bot:2`, …) so the room can tell a bot apart from a real sessionId; everything downstream (dash, collision, shatter-KO, Ougi firing) runs through the exact same sim path a human's commands do — bots are just another source of `SimCommand`s and a `charging` flag.
- `ArenaRoom.ts`: `PlayerState` gained `isBot` for client-side labeling. `startMatch()` (used for both a fresh start and rematch) now: clears every existing bot, gives real joined players priority for the up-to-4 active slots in `joinOrder` order (recomputing `spectating` from scratch rather than trusting stale flags), then fills whatever's left with newly created bots.
