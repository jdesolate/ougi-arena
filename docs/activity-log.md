## S8 — Bots: dash-at-nearest AI, yield-to-human, practice mode (2026-07-23)

- Moved `laneDistance` (Cross Slash's beam-reach math) from `ougi.ts` into `math.ts` as a shared, exported helper — the bot AI needed the same "how far can I go along this axis before hitting a wall/obstacle" logic and duplicating it would have let the two drift apart.
- New `packages/server/src/bot.ts`: `decideBotCommand(state, ninja)` — dash-at-nearest AI, one decision per simulation callback (same cadence real client input arrives at). A bot holds (`charging: true`) until its TP covers at least half a full-power dash, fires its Ougi outright the instant the meter is full, and otherwise picks whichever of the two candidate cardinal axes toward the nearest live target has clearer line-of-sight via `laneDistance` (basic obstacle avoidance) before releasing a `LaunchCommand` at that axis and power. ## S12 — Arena grid in the sim: cell-snapped dashes, per-obstacle HP, ASCII map parser (2026-07-25)

- **Dash destinations snap, positions don't.** `applyLaunch` caps the requested reach by TP as before, then resolves it to a cell centre along the launch axis, **clamping down** — a 2.5-cell drag lands 2 cells away and pays for 2, because rounding up would let a dash outspend its tank. That one choice is what makes the whole thing self-correcting: a ninja parked off-grid (hard-stopped against a pillar, flung by Shockwave, bumped in an idle collision) is pulled back onto a centre by its very next dash on that axis, with no realignment logic and no way to wedge between cells. The corollary, worth knowing: since movement is axis-locked, a horizontal dash only re-aligns `x` — the off-grid `y` persists until a vertical dash. `dashDistanceFor` is exported so the client's aim preview draws the exact landing the sim will resolve rather than an approximation, and the 0ms dash SFX is gated on it too (a sub-cell drag is now a legitimate no-op).

## S14 — Weapons in the sim: cell patterns, cooldowns, facing (2026-07-27)

- **`shared/weapon.ts`** mirrors `ougi.ts`'s shape: `WeaponDefinition { id, name, description, cells, damage, cooldownTicks }`, a `WEAPONS` table, and pure sim functions. A pattern is a list of `{ forward, lateral }` **cell offsets measured in `CELL`** — the same quantum S12 put in the map and the dash, so "one box in front" is literal. Kunai 1 cell / 8 dmg / 0.3s, fan 3 cells (front + both diagonals) / 12 / 0.5s, longsword 2 cells deep / 20 / 0.9s. Sustained damage is deliberately close across the three (~24–27 dps); reach, coverage and burst are what actually differ.
- **The pattern is measured from the attacker's *cell*, not its position** (`snapToCellCentre` on both axes). That's the one decision everything else falls out of: a ninja hard-stopped mid-cell against a pillar still swings at grid-aligned tiles, so an attack box is exactly an obstacle box and the client can highlight the tiles the sim will resolve. `attackCells()` is exported for that.
- **Cover blocks a lane.** `attackCells` walks the pattern nearest-first and drops every cell beyond a wall, a pillar or a *live* destructible in the same `lateral` — whether or not the swing goes on to break it, matching the dash's hard-stop-on-contact rule. Only the longsword has depth today, but reaching through a pillar would have been obviously wrong the first time anyone saw it. Cells are measured against the pre-swing grid (the same discipline S7's Cross Slash needed), so one lane's break can't lengthen another. A test asserts every authored pattern is nearest-first, since the blocking logic depends on that order.
- Damage runs through the existing `damageNinja`, so weapons charge SP by damage dealt and KO at 0 HP for free — **dash-shatter stays the only instant kill** (FR-9/FR-23). Obstacle damage reuses `obstacleHit`/`obstacleDestroyed`; `impact` is now documented as 0 for a weapon hit, since a swing has no closing speed, rather than inventing a parallel event.
- `NinjaState` gains `weaponId`, `facingX`/`facingY` and `attackCooldown`; `AttackCommand` joins the `SimCommand` union and `ninjaAttacked` joins `SimEvent` (so a client can animate a swing it didn't predict). Facing is set by whichever of a dash or a swing came last, so a dash aims the next swing; a zero-direction attack reuses it instead of eating the input. An attack during cooldown is **dropped, not buffered** (FR-24) — buffering makes latency read as input lag. Cooldown decrements in the same per-tick loop as invulnerability, and resets on KO and respawn alongside the other per-life state.
- Attacks cost no TP; a body straddling two cells of one swing takes damage once; the attacker never hits itself.
- New `weapon.test.ts` (29): the table's invariants, per-weapon reach/coverage, diagonal snapping, off-grid cell measurement, cover blocking a lane but not its neighbours, a wall cell still spending the swing, chip-then-destroy on a crate, the full cooldown window, facing from both sources, KO-at-0 vs. survive-at-full, invulnerable targets, and a 900-tick determinism run scripting dashes and swings together. **106 shared tests (was 77)**, typecheck and lint pass across all three packages.
- **No live verification, and deliberately so** — S14 is sim-only per the session table, so nothing here is wired to a message handler or a renderer yet and there is nothing a browser could show. Tests and review are the whole confidence story this session; S15 is where it becomes observable.
- **Next:** S15 — Weapons, player-facing: tap-to-swing input + 0ms swing animation, per-weapon effects/SFX, weapon pick in character select, cooldown on the HUD, bots swing when a target is in range. Model: Sonnet.
- **Open issues (S14):** (1) Nothing is wired: the server has no `"attack"` message, no `weaponId` join option, and syncs neither `facing` nor `attackCooldown`; `createSimState`'s `weaponIds` argument is unused by `ArenaRoom`, so every ninja is currently a kunai. All of that is S15's scope, but it means weapons are unreachable in a running match right now. (2) Damage and cooldown numbers are first-pass and have never been felt — the fan hitting three targets for 12 each may be the strongest of the three in a crowd, and the kunai's 0.3s cooldown may read as spam. Both are single-line table edits. (3) Cover blocks by testing the *cell centre* for a wall/obstacle, which is exact for cell-sized geometry but would need a real overlap test if a map ever authored a box that doesn't fill its cell. (4) Attacking mid-dash is allowed and turns the sprite's facing away from its travel direction; harmless in the sim, but S15 should decide whether that looks wrong.

## S16 — Deploy prep: env config, CORS allowlist, cold-start UX, mobile pass (2026-07-27)

- **Two env vars connect the two hosts, and nothing else does.** Client: `VITE_SERVER_URL` (accepts the `https://` form you copy out of a dashboard and converts it to `wss://`, trailing slash stripped), falling back to `ws://localhost:2567` so local dev needs no env at all; typed via a hand-written `vite-env.d.ts` rather than `vite/client`, since the client tsconfig pins `types: []`. Server: `ALLOWED_ORIGINS`, comma-separated, unset meaning allow-anything for dev.

## S23 — Fan knockback in the sim (2026-07-28)

- **`WeaponDefinition` gained `knockbackCells`** (fan 1 via `FAN_KNOCKBACK_CELLS`, kunai and longsword 0), so "does this weapon move you" is a table edit like damage and cooldown. `attack()` calls a new `applyKnockback` right after `damageNinja` on every hit victim: it sets `vx/vy` to `WEAPON_KNOCKBACK_SPEED` (aliased to Shockwave's, so being shoved reads the same whatever shoved you), loads `dashBudget` with the push distance and leaves `dashLethal` false — the Shockwave precedent, which means walls, pillars and destructibles hard-stop the push for free through the existing `stopAtContact` path, and being flung can never shatter whoever you land on.
- **The push is measured to a cell centre, not by adding a cell.** New `grid.ts` helper `cellPushDistance(from, dir, cells, origin)` returns the distance to the centre of the cell `cells` away from the *containing* cell, so a victim parked off-grid (pillar hard-stop, an earlier fling) is realigned by the shove instead of carrying its offset along. Unlike `snapDashDistance` it never clamps down — a knockback isn't paid for out of TP — so the real travel is anywhere in `[CELL/2, 3·CELL/2)`. Only the push axis realigns; the perpendicular offset persists, same as a dash.
- **Knockback rides its own event, `ninjaKnockback` (`targetId`, `sourceId`, `dirX/dirY`, `distance`)**, rather than extra fields on `ninjaDamaged` — `damage.ts` is shared with Ougis and had no business knowing about weapons. Both fire in the same tick, and `distance` is what the push was *granted*, before cover cuts it short, which is what S24's slide animation wants. The client's `handleSimEvents` switch has no `default`, so the new type is inert until S24 handles it.
- Every cell of a pattern pushes along the swing direction, not outward from the attacker, so a fan's two diagonal victims are swept the same way as the one in front — one motion rather than a starburst. Fan's card description now mentions the shove.
- **Emergent, and kept:** a victim shoved into a crate deals the usual impact damage to it (520 speed × 0.22 = 114 vs `CRATE_HP` 100), so a fan can break cover through a body. Identical to Shockwave's fling since S7, credited to the victim in the `obstacleDestroyed` event; a test pins it.
